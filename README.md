### Selected work

**[Shiftseal](https://sushilraj.dev/shiftseal/)**: proof of work for shift workers. Tamper-evident shift records, overtime claims checked against evidence, and AI answers that are verified before a worker sees them. Gemini on Vertex AI, Cloud Run, Firestore. Top 10 at Prompt Wars 2026 (UN SDG 8), with an AI evaluation score of 96.51/100.
[Live app](https://shiftseal-872210045960.asia-south1.run.app) · [Code](https://github.com/SushilRaj0177/shiftseal)

**[Sumi](https://sushilraj.dev/sumi/)**: an Android app for the 612 JLPT kanji (N5 to N3), with stroke-order practice and a pen pal who writes using only the kanji you've learned. Every reading is checked against JMdict on the device. Kotlin, Jetpack Compose, FSRS. In closed beta on Google Play.

**[Kehai Engine](https://github.com/SushilRaj0177/Kehai-Engine)**: geospatially verified QR attendance for events and classrooms, with rotating signed QR codes, geofenced check-ins and real-time dashboards. Next.js, Express, Postgres. English and Japanese.

**[Kehai QR Studio](https://kehai-qr-studio.vercel.app)**: design QR codes that actually scan. Every download is verified by decoding the exact image. Runs entirely in the browser, offline. English and Japanese.

**[sushilraj.dev](https://sushilraj.dev)**: my personal site, floating on a real-time WebGL fluid simulation that follows your cursor or finger.
